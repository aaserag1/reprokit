# Launch research notes

## Hacker News / Show HN

- A Show HN must be something people can try; landing pages and sign-up pages are off topic.
- The maker should have worked on the project and be available to discuss it.
- Make it easy to try without barriers; use a `Show HN:` title.
- Do not ask friends to upvote or comment.

Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html

## Reddit

- Treat Reddit as a community rather than a link-distribution channel.
- Be transparent about being the project author, read each subreddit’s rules, and ask moderators when uncertain.
- Do not ask for votes, buy votes, use alternate accounts, spam private messages, or post mostly self-promotional links.
- Participate in relevant discussion and respond to questions.

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/reddit.com/wiki/selfpromotion/
